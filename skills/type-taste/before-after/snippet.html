Ghost cursors in component demos

18 November 2026 · 4 min read

A screenshot of a toggle shows two states. It can’t show the moment in between, and that moment is the reason the demo exists. So I record a real cursor moving through the real DOM and replay it on the page. When the reader moves their own mouse, the recording stops and hands over.

How it works

The engine replays pointer events through springs, so timing survives re-renders. Call replay(recording) to start it. Playback pauses when the demo scrolls offscreen and never starts under reduced motion. A recording is a JSON array of pointer events, and the format fits on one screen.
